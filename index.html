Reshinth Adithyan

Research Engineer at Stability AI · London

I'm a Research Engineer at Stability AI, based in London, on the Code.AI team — where I predominantly work on the post-training of LLMs; previously I worked on the large-scale pre-training of CodeLMs. I helped build the Stable Code suite of models and now do extensive research on LLM×RL post-training. Outside of work, I enjoy Impressionist painting and writing about music.

I was also a core member of Code.AI, an open-source community of researchers and engineers working on code intelligence and ML-aided code completion, where I was a major contributor to Code Clippy — one of the first open-source code language models.
